CHPP Product License Agreement
1.1 Any software program, tool, service or website that has been specifically purposed for accessing and handling Hattrick data needs to obtain a CHPP License. We refer to any licensed application as a "CHPP product". By requesting a CHPP license for your product, you commit to following these rules.
Requesting a CHPP license
2.1 Request a development license for each individual product by briefly describing what you want to develop and how it will benefit the Hattrick users. This must be done in English. When approved, you may start development as well as test using live data from your personal Hattrick team account.
During development, you need to submit a detailed functional description of the product in progress. Your product will not be able to leave development status until your functional description has been approved. When it is approved it can then be released to end users and published in the Hattrick CHPP portal.
Approval and Termination
3.1 Each CHPP Product License is granted at the discretion of Hattrick Holdings Limited. This means Hattrick can withdraw a License at any time. This may be done if a product if found to violate the terms of this Agreement, or otherwise deemed necessary.
Functional Description
4.1 To obtain a product license, it is necessary to provide a functional description which details what Hattrick resources will be used and what they will be used for. Once a License has been given, and a product published, you should keep the functional description up to date. If you add or change functions, notify the CHPP admins through your product management page on Hattrick. It is your responsibility to make sure your product is compliant with the CHPP rules at all times.
Access to Hattrick
5.1 It is forbidden to ask the user for his or her login name and/or password for any purpose. All products must use Hattrick's OAuth authentication scheme. Excepted from the OAuth rule are browser plugins that may access data directly through user sessions. Products given CHPP status before November 2010 may continue using the previous authentication scheme until June 2011.
5.2 Your product may not share data with any other application, web site or service (CHPP or otherwise) without our explicit consent. If your product needs to share data, contact us for approval.
5.3 Your product may not automate access to Hattrick without our explicit consent. If you want your product to automate access to Hattrick, contact us for approval.
5.4 Your product must use Hattrick data in a correct and economical way. Data may never be accessed by other means than through our dedicated CHPP XML interface. Your product should only download the minimum amount of data necessary for your product to run. Automated download of data may not be done more than once a day per account or Hattrick page. These guidelines may change over time, but you will be informed about changes accordingly. We may also specify limits for individual products if necessary.
Scope of products
6.1 Any product that exploit game bugs, or that enables users to cheat or break the game rules are not allowed. This includes products that give users undue advantages in gameplay or that enable users to ruin the gameplay of other users.
6.2 XML data and access to site functionality is made available at Hattrick's discretion. We know that generous access spurs creativity among developers, but we also recognize that some areas of the site may never be suitable for access to all developers and products. Some data may be reserved for products developed by Hattrick or Hattrick partners. Other data may be limited to certain user groups, such as Supporter users. CHPP developers are encouraged to make use of such data to create features that by extension make Hattrick Supporter more attractive.
6.3 CHPP products are non-exclusive - this means that you may try to improve upon concepts or ideas developed by others CHPP developers. We don't plan to police what products are more innovative than others, but we would ask everyone to take a good hard look at what is already out there before you embark on a project, and to try to think of ways to improve on an idea rather than just copy it, if something similar is already out there.
We may restrict licenses to performance-demanding products such as stats or NT trackers if identical services are already provided by other CHPPs. We may provide rating tools and other filters that will be designed to promote high-quality products before derivative, low-quality or badly supported products.
6.4 CHPP products are meant to be used by the Hattrick community as a whole. It is not allowed to create products that only a subset of users are allowed to use. The only exception to this is products that are built for a specific federation or league. To help users discover existing products we require that all approved CHPP products must be published in the CHPP portal shortly after approval. Products that are not published risk having their approval removed or being force-published.
Trademarks and copyrights
7.1 If your product is intended for release within third party distribution channels (for example through Apple, Mozilla, or Google channels) please declare this when you request your Product License. We may ask you to change the name if there is a risk of confusion it with being an "official" Hattrick product, as we want to retain the possibility of releasing such products in the future.
7.2 With the CHPP license, you may use the specific CHPP logos in your product. However, you may not use the Hattrick logo, illustrations or other elements of the web site, without our explicit approval. It's worth asking for it if you want it, though - we usually do not see a problem with this, but we have to be formal about this otherwise our lawyer will be upset with us.
Privacy policy
8.1 Your product must have a published privacy policy - and you must follow it! It should not offer the users less protection than the Hattrick privacy policy, so have a look at that one if you want ideas for your own.
CHPP and other Hattrick services
9.1 It's very important that your product does not mimic, replace, undermine, or cannibalize in any way on Hattrick Supporter, or any other premium service of Hattrick. If your product contains functions similar to ones found in Hattrick Supporter, they must be specified clearly in your functional description and given approval. We don't want to restrict your creative freedom to develop stuff that makes Hattrick more enjoyable for users, but everyone involved in CHPP must understand and accept that all of Hattrick is a symbiotic relationship made possible by our Supporter members.
Commercial CHPP products
10.1 If your product is paid for - that is, if you are charging for all of it or for any part of it, including non-vital upgrades - you must say so when requesting your license. We allow CHPP developers to charge for their stuff, and we'll highlight this in the CHPP portal on Hattrick.org. Since money is involved we want it to be made clear to the users that Hattrick cannot take responsibility for problems caused by you, such as the product not working or it being taken out of development. If you charge for your product, you must tell your users also on your side that they are not paying Hattrick for the service, and that Hattrick can take no responsibility for their future access to your product.
