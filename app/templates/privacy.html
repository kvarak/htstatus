Privacy policy

{{ apptitle }} is a free hobby project for Hattrick managers, run by the Hattrick user Kvarak. It is an approved CHPP product: it reads your Hattrick data through Hattrick's official interface (CHPP), with your permission. Questions: send a Hattrick mail (HT-mail) to Kvarak.

What is stored, and why
  • Your account: your Hattrick user id and login name, your {{ apptitle }} username and password (hashed), and the access key Hattrick gives us when you log in. The key only lets {{ apptitle }} read your data; it can't change anything in your team. We never see or store your Hattrick password.
  • Your teams: what Hattrick sends when you log in or click "Update data": your teams, your players (with their history, so you can see how they develop), your matches with line-ups and ratings, training, your series table and bids on your players. For the scouting of your next matches we also keep recent results of your opponents.
  • What you add: player groups, column and display choices, and feedback posts, comments and votes.
  • Usage: when you last logged in, updated and used the site, and how often you open each page and the guided tours. This shows which features are used.
  • Errors and logs: when something breaks, the error with the page, browser and your user id, so it can be fixed. The web server keeps short-lived request logs (IP address, page, time).
Cookies

A login cookie keeps you logged in and is removed when you log out. If you change the language or the skill level format in Settings, that choice is kept in a cookie too. {% if config.GOATCOUNTER_URL %} Page views are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. {% endif %}

Who sees it

Your team and player data is shown only to you. Feedback posts and comments are visible to other logged-in users. The site admin can see the list of users and their usage, to run the site. Nothing is sold or shared with other sites or apps, and public pages never show team or player data.

How long

Your data stays as long as you have an account. Database backups are kept for about a month and then deleted.

Delete your account
  1. Log in, open Settings and click "Delete my account". This removes your account, teams, players, matches, groups, settings and feedback right away. A match against another {{ apptitle }} user's team stays for that user, and error reports stay without your user id.
  2. On Hattrick, go to My Hattrick → Preferences → CHPP products and click "Remove permission" next to {{ apptitle }}. This withdraws the access key; logging out of {{ apptitle }} doesn't.

Changes to this policy are listed on the changes page.
