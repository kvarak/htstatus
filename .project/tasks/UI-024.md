# UI-024: Flat "football poster" redesign

## Problem

The current look (neutral grays, green + slate accents, tinted background photo) is clean but anonymous. The illustrations in `app/static/_more/` (gitignored, local only) have the feeling we want: flat, warm, a bit retro, clearly football.

## Inspiration (from `app/static/_more/`)

- **player.jpg** (main reference): a mosaic of flat tiles in dark brown, pitch green and sand; cream-white kit, ball, goal, trophy, scoreboard and tactics-board icons; a small "SOCCER" type tile
- **Lifestyle 02.jpg**: the same tiled-poster layout with brown, sand, red and orange tiles
- **Soccer 01.jpg**: two-color screen-print style (orange + dark plum) with a light grain texture
- **Ball 04.jpg**: a pattern of flat kit icons (shirts, boots, goals, trophies, stars) in two greens
- **ms_00000581 / match.jpg**: flat striped pitch with white lines; match.jpg also has a "TEAM vs TEAM" scoreboard bar
- **stadium.png, football002.jpg**: flat vector stadium, pitch, whistle, cards, trophy
- **background.jpg** (blue low-poly) does not fit the rest; leave it out

## Direction

- **Palette** (tokens in `base.html` `:root`, dark mode in `dark-mode.css`):
  - espresso brown (about `#3b2a26`): navbar, headers, dark tiles
  - pitch green (about `#5f8a3a`): primary/our team; replaces the current green
  - sand/khaki (about `#c9bfa8`): page background and muted tiles
  - cream (about `#f4efe4`): cards and text on dark
  - one warm accent (orange about `#e8892b`) for highlights and links instead of slate blue; red only for errors and bad values
- **Style**:
  - flat solid colors, no gradients, no shadows or very small ones, square or slightly rounded corners
  - the dashboard and stats read as a poster mosaic: tiles of different sizes and colors
  - flat icons for headings (ball, boot, trophy, whistle, scoreboard) instead of emoji
- **Background**: replace the tinted photo with a subtle sand pattern of flat kit icons (like Ball 04), or plain sand
- **Football touches**:
  - the formations pitch uses flat stripes and white lines (ms_00000581)
  - match headers use a "Home vs Away" scoreboard bar (match.jpg)
  - big numbers (scores, stats) in a scoreboard-style font
- **Dark mode**: brown becomes the surface, sand becomes muted text, and the green and orange accents stay

## Acceptance criteria

- One palette and style across all pages (`theme.css`, `dark-mode.css`); charts take their colors from `chartColor()` (country charts keep flag colors)
- Text contrast stays WCAG AA in light and dark mode
- Any images or icons used are free to use (check the license), committed in a small web size, and the `_more/` originals are not committed
- `make test-all` passes; check pages in the browser in both modes
