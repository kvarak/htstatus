# Releases

**Purpose**: User-focused summaries of new features and improvements in HattrickPlanner
**Audience**: Hattrick managers who want to understand what's new without technical details
**Format**: Only versions with user-facing changes that you'll actually notice

*For technical details, see [RELEASES-INTERNAL.md](RELEASES-INTERNAL.md)*

## 3.25 - November 1, 2026

- **Football poster look**: flat brown, green, sand and orange tiles, a scoreboard font and football icons, also in dark mode
- **New home page**: one tile per team with direct links to all its pages
- **Scoreboards**: matches start with a "Home 2 1 Away" bar; stats and series show their key numbers as big tiles
- **Striped pitch** in the formation tester
- **Fixes**: the spinning ball no longer sticks after Back, and the footer shows the full version (3.25.0)

*HattrickPlanner now looks like a football poster*

## 3.24 - November 1, 2026

- **One look on every page**, plus a dark mode (🌙/☀️) that follows your system setting
- **Hattrick in your language**: skills, levels, specialties and traits use Hattrick's own words; show levels as words, numbers or both (Settings)
- **Series page**: league table, form, next round and your remaining matches
- **Current bid** column and popup details for transfer-listed players
- **New columns** show up in your table automatically
- **Better stats**: squad by best position, country counts and percentages, club-only top scorers
- **Fixes**: sold players leave your team, "Assign to group" works, a failed Update says why, and fewer crashes

*A consistent look in your own Hattrick language*

## 3.23 - February 6, 2026

- **Enhanced Player Management**: Complete overhaul of player management with powerful new tools
  - **Bulk Player Actions**: Select multiple players and assign them to teams or roles simultaneously
  - **Advanced Player Filtering**: Find exactly the players you want with position, skill level, and team filters
  - **Player Comparison Tool**: Compare multiple players side-by-side with visual skill charts
  - **Smart Table Interactions**: Enhanced player table with sorting, filtering, and bulk selection
  - **Real-time Search**: Instantly filter through your players as you type
- **Improved User Experience**: Streamlined workflows for managing large player rosters
  - Loading indicators and error handling for smooth operation
  - Form validation to prevent common mistakes
  - Responsive design that works on all screen sizes
- **Visual Analytics**: Radar charts help you quickly compare player skills and make informed decisions

*Perfect for managers with large squads who need efficient player management tools*

## 3.22 - February 5, 2026

- **Comprehensive Match Analytics**: Deep dive into your match performance with detailed statistics
  - **Possession Data**: See ball possession percentages for each half - track your team's control
  - **Chance Breakdown**: View scoring opportunities by position (left, center, right, set pieces, special events)
  - **Positional Ratings**: Detailed ratings for defense and attack across all field positions
  - **Match Environment**: Arena attendance, weather conditions, referee information
  - **Formations & Tactics**: See exactly how both teams lined up and what tactics were used
- **Redesigned Matches Page**: Clean card-based layout with match history sidebar and detailed analytics panel
  - Win/Draw/Loss badges for quick match result scanning
  - Click any match to see full analytics and tactical breakdown
  - Upcoming matches show opponent history to help you prepare
- **Archive Download**: Fetch historical match data from past seasons for long-term analysis
- **Visual Performance Indicators**: Color-coded ratings and gradient bars make it easy to spot strengths and weaknesses at a glance

*Note: Some match features still in development - look for the "Work in Progress" badge*
